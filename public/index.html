a country map for hard days

Every hard feeling is a place you pass through. Every lane leads home.

Worry that keeps you up at 3am. Grey mornings. Exams, deadlines and an inbox with teeth. An hour stuck behind a tractor with a meltdown in the back seat. They feel like separate problems. On this map they are lanes in the same valley, and every one of them climbs towards a cottage on the hill with the kettle on. Tap a place to read it, or tell us where you are and we will walk you home.
