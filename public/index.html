A motorway map for hard days

Every hard feeling is a junction.
All roads lead home.

Anxiety at 3am. A grey morning you cannot get out of bed for. Exams, deadlines, an inbox with teeth. Two hours nose to tail on the ring road with a meltdown in the back seat. They feel like separate problems. On this map they are motorways, and every one of them is driving you somewhere. Tap a sign to read it, or put your junction into the satnav and watch the road take you home.
