Every feeling is a stop.
None of them is the end of the line.

Anxiety, grey days, exam pressure, an hour stuck on the ring road, a toddler flat out on the supermarket floor. They feel like separate problems. On this map they are stations on one network, with interchanges between them and trains that keep moving. Tap any station to see what it feels like and the way through, or plan a journey from where you are to where you would like to be.
