Worries, cross days, sad days, scary nights and big changes can all feel like stormy seas. On this map, every feeling is an island you can sail past, every sea monster can be tamed, and every route ends at treasure: feeling calm, loved, safe and secure. Tap an island or a monster to find out about it, or plan a voyage from where you are today.

For grown-ups

The map is built for children aged 8 to 13, but it works best with a grown-up alongside. Here is how to get the most from it at home or in class.

Ask where, not why

Which island are you on today? is far easier to answer than Why are you upset? Let them point. You do not need to fix anything straight away. Knowing where they are is the first step.

Monsters make it talkable

Turning a big feeling into a sea monster puts a little distance between the child and the feeling. Is the Kraken grabbing you again? lands very differently from Stop worrying. The child is the captain. The monster is just something on the voyage.

Tame, never fight

Every monster on the map gets tamed, not defeated. That is deliberate. Feelings are not enemies, and children who learn to calm a feeling cope far better than children who learn to hide it.

Be the lighthouse

The Trusted Grown-Up Lighthouse sits on the Golden Current because every route passes it. If a child seems stuck on the same island for more than a couple of weeks, or a wobble starts getting in the way of sleep, school or friendships, talk to their GP or school.
