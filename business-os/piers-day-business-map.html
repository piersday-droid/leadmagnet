The people

One person pays. Another is helped. The whole house changes.

Who calls

The worried parent

Usually a middle-class mum or dad who wants their child to do as well as possible. Already tried the GP and been sent to a six to eight month waiting list. Often does not realise how bad things are until late. Busiest in March, April and May, when exams bite.

Who is helped

The young man in transition

Aged 14 to 25. Anxious or low, hiding in his bedroom, avoiding his year group. Or older: degree done, no job, frustrated, leaning on weed, cocaine or alcohol to get through the day. Mostly needs someone to talk to who does not judge him.

Before: on the first call
I’m worried about my son. The doctor keeps referring me to MIND, and the waiting list is six to eight months. You are my last hope.
After: a few weeks later
He’s back on form, like his old self again. We’re enjoying family time again. Since the last session I’ve been able to sleep soundly again, knowing all my family are well.
