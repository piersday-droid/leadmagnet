Questions people ask

Before you book

How many sessions will I need?

One. The session is two hours so we have time to do the job properly in a single visit. If you smoke again within six months, you come back for a second session free of charge. In my experience only around one person in twelve takes me up on that.

Will I lose control?

No. You are awake, aware and in charge the whole time. Hypnosis cannot make you do anything you do not want to do, which is exactly why it works best when you have already decided to stop.

Does it work online?

Yes. Online sessions by video work just as well as sessions in the clinic, and you get to stay in your own chair afterwards. I see people from all over the UK and abroad this way.

What if I have tried everything else?

Most people who come to me have. Patches, gum, cold turkey, willpower. Those deal with the nicotine. The session deals with the habit and the thinking, which is the part that usually pulls people back.

Will I put on weight?

Ask me about it on the call. I spent years helping people with weight and food cravings, so we can talk about what worries you before the session.
